The product

What CompCheck is

CompCheck is for the research that happens before a resale price is set. You start with an item, look at comparable listings, and keep that context together while you choose a price.

One research view

Review the listings you are treating as comps alongside the item, instead of reconstructing them from tabs and notes.

Room for judgment

Condition, completeness, and listing details matter as much as the number. CompCheck is a place to look at those together.

A pricing step

Decide what to charge while the comparisons are still in front of you. CompCheck does not choose the price for you.

The problem

Comps research falls apart when it lives in tabs.

Online resellers often check several listings, then try to remember which ones were actually similar. When it is time to set a price, the comparison is fuzzy.

What gets in the way

  • Comparable listings end up spread across tabs, screenshots, and notes.
  • It is easy to forget which listings were truly similar.
  • The price gets set after the comparison is already out of sight.

What CompCheck is for

  • Reviewing comps in the same place as the item you are pricing.
  • Reading condition and listing details next to the comparison.
  • Choosing a price while that research is still in view.

Workflow

How it works

A straightforward research flow. This is the high-level path. It is not an automated pricing promise.

  1. 1

    Start with the item

    Begin with something you own or something you are thinking about buying.

  2. 2

    Review comparable listings

    Look at listings that resemble the item. Pay attention to condition and details, not only price.

  3. 3

    Decide what to charge

    Set your price with those comparisons still available to you.

You remain responsible for the price, the listing, and any fees. CompCheck does not sell your items and does not promise a particular result.

Audience

Who it is for

CompCheck is for people who price resale goods and want comparable listings in front of them before they decide.

  • Resellers who sell used, vintage, or one-of-a-kind items online.
  • Sellers who check comparable listings before they buy inventory.
  • Anyone who wants pricing research kept with the item, not lost in browser history.

CompCheck is a research aid. It is not a marketplace, and it is not a substitute for reading a listing carefully.

Why use it

Why a reseller would use it

Buying and listing both need comps

The same questions show up when you source an item and when you publish a price. A comps tool is for both moments.

These goods do not have a shelf price

Used and unique items vary. Looking at similar listings is how resellers build a price they can explain.

Memory is a weak record

A price is easier to stand behind when you can still see the listings you treated as comps.
