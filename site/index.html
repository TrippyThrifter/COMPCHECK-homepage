The product

What CompCheck is

CompCheck is for people who resell goods online and set prices from comparable sales. A comp is a comparable item: close enough to the piece you have that its price is useful context.

The tool is built around a practical question. What have similar items been priced at, and what will you charge for this one?

The problem

Pricing is harder when the research is scattered

Resellers often look at similar listings and past sales before choosing a price. That comparison is the useful part. It gets clumsy when the comps live in separate tabs, screenshots, and memory.

CompCheck is meant to hold that research together, so the price you publish has a reference point you can come back to.

Workflow

How it works

At a high level, CompCheck supports three steps. You stay in control of the price.

  1. 01

    Describe the item

    Start with what you are pricing. Note the details that change value, such as condition, size, or a feature that makes the piece different.

  2. 02

    Review comps

    Look at comparable sales and listings before you commit to a number. Sold prices and current asking prices answer different questions, so keep them distinct.

  3. 03

    Choose your price

    Use the comps as context, then decide what you will charge. CompCheck organizes the research. It does not choose the price for you.

Audience

Who it’s for

CompCheck is a fit when comparable sales are already part of how you price.

  • Online resellers

    You buy and sell goods online, and you want similar items nearby when you decide what to charge.

  • Sellers who research first

    You already compare listings before you publish a price, and you want that step to be repeatable.

  • People replacing scattered notes

    You would rather keep comps in one tool than reconstruct them from browser history later.

Reasons

Why a reseller would use it

  • Comps in one place

    Review the sales and listings that informed a price without hunting through old tabs.

  • A routine before you list

    Item, comps, price. The same sequence can precede each listing you prepare.

  • Judgment stays with you

    The tool is there for context. Two similar items can still deserve different prices, and you make that call.

What CompCheck does not do

  • It does not guarantee a sale, a profit, or a correct price.
  • It does not replace your knowledge of the item in front of you.
  • It does not turn sample figures on this page into a live valuation.
  • This marketing site does not take payment, store an account, or open a private application.

Questions

    No. You choose what to charge after you review comps. Nothing on this site promises what an item will sell for.
